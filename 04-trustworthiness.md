# Trustworthiness

Trustworthiness is the foundation of E-E-A-T and the most important of the four: whether your content is honest and accurate, and whether your site is safe and reliable. The other three matter only on top of trust; experience, expertise, and authority count for little if the content cannot be trusted.

## Trust is the foundation

Trustworthiness underpins the rest, because experience, expertise, and authoritativeness are valuable only if the content is also honest and accurate. A knowledgeable, experienced, recognized source that is dishonest or misleading fails the whole framework. Trust is treated as the central quality, the one the others support rather than substitute for. Without it, the rest does not save the content.

## Accuracy and honesty

Trustworthy content is accurate, supported where claims need support, honest about what is known and unknown, and free of misleading framing. It does not overstate, fabricate, or deceive. It corrects errors and updates when things change. These honesty markers signal to readers and systems that the content can be relied on. Accuracy and honesty are the substance of trustworthiness, demonstrated in the content itself.

## Site-level trust signals

Trust also operates at the site level. Clear contact information, transparent ownership, honest policies, secure connections, and the absence of deceptive practices all signal a trustworthy site. A reader should be able to tell who is behind the content and how to reach them. These structural trust signals reassure both readers and systems that the site is a legitimate, accountable operation rather than an anonymous or deceptive one. Trustworthiness is shown by the site, not just the words.

## Trust is easily lost

Trust is hard to build and easy to lose. A pattern of accuracy and honesty builds it slowly; a single instance of deception, a fabricated claim, a fake review, a misleading practice, can damage it quickly. This asymmetry means trustworthiness has to be protected consistently, because the cost of a lapse outweighs the gain that prompted it. For a small operator whose reputation is the business, guarding trust is guarding the foundation everything else rests on.
